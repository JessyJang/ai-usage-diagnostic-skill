# V2 Beta scoring calibration

## Core dimensions (same for every deep assessment)
1. Problem framing: task/context/constraints/success criteria.
2. Critical verification: errors, contradictions, checking against sources, controlled revision.
3. Execution and shipping: deliverable actually used or published, not just a draft.
4. Reuse and operations: durable templates, SOPs, skill reuse and maintenance.
5. Feedback and measurement: explicit checks against useful outcomes, qualitative evidence acceptable.
6. Judgment and prioritization: stops unnecessary work, chooses simpler alternatives, scopes effort.

## Evidence grades
- A: independently inspectable deliverable or repeated directly observed behavior.
- B: concrete user-reported example without independent inspection.
- C: broad self-description or claim without examples.
- U: unknown/missing (not scored as failure).
Evidence confidence considers coverage, independence, recency, and consistency; it is not the ability score.

## Anchor-based holistic maturity
1–2: isolated requests, no demonstrated adaptation.
3–4: supplies context and revises individual tasks.
5–6: reliably decomposes and verifies multi-step work; reuse is inconsistent.
7: repeatable workflows plus actual deliverables; limited durable feedback.
8: reliable, context-aware delivery and strong verification; some system gaps remain.
9: repeated reuse, feedback and measurable/observable improvements across actual tasks.
10: exceptionally stable multi-context system with independently supported sustained outcomes; very rare.

A single portfolio screenshot or number of Agents does not prove 8+. Missing revenue evidence is NOT automatic penalty for a hobby user. Display an explicit one-decimal anchored point score when at least one concrete behavioral example exists; distinguish *display granularity* from statistical precision. Mark low-evidence scores as provisional and confidence low. With no concrete example, ask for one rather than inventing a number. The score is not an arithmetic average of the six dimensions.


## Scoring anchors for each dimension (0–10, unknown when unobserved)
- 2: isolated attempt, mostly unstructured; 4: repeats a simple method with some correction; 6: handles multi-step work with situational checking; 8: independently defines, tests and delivers reliably across examples; 10: repeatable cross-context excellence with strong outcome evidence.
- Interpolate between nearest anchors *only when supported by behavior*. Use `未知` when no relevant episode exists. Dimension estimates do not imply validated measurement.

## Score selection worked examples (illustrative hypotheses, NOT empirical results)
- One specific new-user prompt/revision, limited history: 4.5/10 **暂定**, low confidence; actual score depends on what the case demonstrates.
- Many tools but no demonstrated task execution: 3.5/10 **暂定**, low confidence; no automatic advanced rating.
- Repeatedly shipped useful workflows with clear quality checks, reuse unknown: ~7.5/10, medium confidence.
- Durable routine with demonstrated measurable improvements and corrections: ~8.8/10 with sufficiently broad evidence, medium/high confidence.

## Evidence adjustment discipline
- Previously unknown -> independently proven: may affect both confidence and rating.
- Reworded boast -> same evidence: should NOT increase rating.
- A published artifact -> evidence of delivery, NOT necessarily adoption or repeatability.
- Missing sales -> not a penalty unless the user's goal involves selling and validated commercial results are the dimension under study.
