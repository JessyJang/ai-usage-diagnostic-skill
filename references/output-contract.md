# Required output

**AI 使用总分：X.X / 10** (if no concrete event: `证据不足：需要一个真实使用案例`)
**能力等级：... | 证据可信度：高/中/低 | 诊断：正式/暂定**
**证据范围：** direct conversations/artifacts vs reported episodes; accessible time window and missing data.

Six dimensions in order: name | X.X or 未知 | evidence IDs | brief reason. Never compute average.

**证据**: 2–5 observations with A/B/C grades; limitations stated.
**强项**: exactly 2 with evidence IDs.
**瓶颈**: 1–2 with rival causal explanations.
**Red Team**: strongest supported countercase, verdict and refutation.
**为什么不是更高/更低一级**: grounded anchors.
**一个行动 (7–30 天)**: baseline, action, pass/fail, evidence saved, decision rule.
**什么会改变结果**: one counterexample lowering score, one raising; confidence/skill separate.

For follow-up:
`复测基线 | 新增证据 | 事实/信心/能力变化 | 变化原因`.
Provide copyable baseline upon request. No generic identity titles or unsupported percentile ranking.
