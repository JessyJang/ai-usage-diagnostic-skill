# Required user-visible output, adapt length to Fast vs Deep

**AI 使用总分：X.X / 10** (if no concrete event: `证据不足：需要一个真实使用案例`)
**能力等级：... | 证据可信度：高/中/低 | 诊断：正式/暂定**
**证据范围：** direct conversations/artifacts vs reported episodes; accessible time window, missing data.

Six dimensions in fixed order: name | X.X or 未知 | evidence IDs | short reason. Never add a fake average.

**证据**: 2–5 observations with A/B/C source grades; say what evidence cannot establish.
**强项**: exactly 2 backed by IDs.
**瓶颈**: 1–2 with competing cause attribution (user/AI tool/external/unknown); avoid blaming user for outputs of poor model.
**Red Team**: strongest supported challenge; verdict + what refutes it.
**为什么不是更高/更低一级**: distinct anchored behaviors.
**一个行动 (7–30 天)**: baseline; exact action; observable pass/fail; what to record; decision rule; no new tools required.
**什么会改变结果**: one counterexample that could lower it, and one new behavioral observation that could raise it; confidence vs skill changes distinguished.

At repeat assessment append:
`复测基线 | 新增证据 | 事实/信心/能力变化 | 具体变化原因`.
When a user requests a copyable baseline, make one. Avoid default grand declarations, generic identity labels and percentage rankings.
